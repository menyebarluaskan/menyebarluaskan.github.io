Enjo Advance, a creature collection game. Enjo is a mysterious creature that
can transform into more advanced form by eating stuffs in your land. That stuff
is Enjorodients. You help Enjo in your land to grow by helping them eat the
Enjorodients.

![Enjo Gameplay](./gameplay-1.webp)

You also can Adopt new Enjo to collect various Enjo variant because Enjo can
transform based on what they eats.

![Enjo Gameplay 2](./gameplay-2.webp)

Manage your lands, enjos, in a profile page. You can rename your current land
or buy new land for adopting more enjos because each land has a capacity limit!

![Your Profile](./profile-1.webp)
